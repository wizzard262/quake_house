QUAKE MAP - MY HOUSE

beak8v · 1 Nov 1997 ·

Ported with Claude Code so quake map is rendered in the browser with javascript.

Technologies: JavaScript, three.js (WebGL) and GLSL shaders, Quake BSP collision hulls and movement ported to JavaScript, Python (map converter: geometry, textures with the Quake palette, baked lightmaps), GitHub Pages.

Click to play · mouse to look · WASD move · Space jump / swim up · Shift walk · click to shoot (some buttons and doors are shot, like in Quake) · N noclip (fly, Q/E down/up) · R restart · P copy your position · Esc free the mouse
